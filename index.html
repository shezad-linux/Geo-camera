6. Location and Address Accuracy

Geo Camera does not guarantee the accuracy of geographical information. Please read this section carefully before relying on or sharing any location information.

  • GPS coordinates may be inaccurate. Depending on satellite signal, indoor conditions, terrain, and device hardware, reported coordinates may be approximate, stale, or incorrectly recorded.
  • Reverse-geocoded addresses may contain errors. Addresses are derived from coordinates using geocoding data sources. They may be incomplete, outdated, or assigned to the wrong building, street, or locality.
  • Weather and map information may be unavailable or delayed. Weather data, map data, and nearby-place information depend on network connectivity and third-party availability.
  • Environmental conditions and device hardware affect results. Battery level, sensor quality, operating system settings, and network conditions can affect location fixes and related information.
  • Approximate or unavailable states. The application may use a cached or last-known position, indicate approximate accuracy, or show that location or address information is unavailable. An on-screen location is not necessarily a fresh, high-accuracy fix.
Do not rely on Geo Camera for safety-critical decisionsDo not use Geo Camera as a substitute for professional mapping, navigation, emergency, legal, or surveying services. Verify location information independently before acting on it, especially before sharing photographs publicly.
