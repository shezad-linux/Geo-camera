Privacy Policy

Last updated 20 November 2026

This policy explains how the publisher of GPS Map Camera: Geo Tagging handles information when you use the Android app.

Camera, photos and location

The app uses camera access to take photos. With your permission, it uses precise or approximate location for geotagging and watermarks. Photos may include coordinates, estimated addresses, timestamps or weather in visible watermarks and metadata. The app stores photos and related records on your device and may save a copy to your Android photo gallery. Sharing or exporting a photo can disclose its embedded location and other details to the app or recipient you choose.

If you use online address or weather lookup, location coordinates and ordinary request information, such as your IP address, may be sent to the services that return the address or weather. The app can also use local data and available Android location and geocoding services. Weather lookup is provided by Open-Meteo.

Advertising and consent

The app uses Google AdMob and may serve ads through Unity Ads mediation. These services may process device or advertising identifiers, IP addresses, ad interactions and diagnostic information for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. An IP address may be used to estimate a general location. Ad partner processing and personalization depend on your choices, device settings and the applicable ad-serving rules.

Where Google’s consent form is required, you can accept, decline or manage your choices. To change or withdraw consent, open the app and go to Settings → Advertisements → Privacy. The Privacy button appears when Google’s consent platform reports that privacy options are required. You can also manage advertising identifiers through Android’s privacy settings.

For details, see Google’s Privacy Policy and Unity’s app user privacy policy.

Analytics and crash reports

Firebase Analytics helps measure app use and feature interactions. Firebase Crashlytics helps diagnose crashes and stability problems. These services may process app and device information, usage events, installation identifiers, crash traces and diagnostic data. See Firebase privacy and security information.

Retention, deletion and your choices

Local photos and records remain on your device until deleted or removed through Android storage controls. Use Gallery to manage local photo records and the device’s photo app to check for and delete any separate copies. Clearing app storage or uninstalling the app may remove app-held data. Copies exported, shared or saved to shared storage may remain. Third-party services retain information under their own policies and settings.

You can change camera and location permissions in Android Settings. If you have questions about your data or wish to exercise privacy rights that apply to you, use the contact below. Requests concerning copies you have shared may also need to be directed to the recipient or service holding the copy.

Children and policy updates

The app is not directed to children. If you believe a child’s information has been provided inappropriately, contact us. We post changes to this policy on this page and update the date above.
