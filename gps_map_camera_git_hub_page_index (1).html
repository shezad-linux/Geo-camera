Privacy Policy — GPS Map Camera
Effective date: 16 Nov 2025

1. Introduction
GPS Map Camera ("the App") is developed by Cityline Developers. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how it is stored, and the rights available to users.

2. Information We Collect
• Photos & Videos: Media you capture inside the app is stored locally. Cloud sync is optional.
• GPS Location Data: Used to tag media with coordinates, generate map views, and display addresses.
• Weather Data: Fetched based on your location when weather tagging is enabled.
• Device Info & Diagnostics: Crash logs, performance data, and basic device identifiers.

3. How We Use Your Information
• To tag photos/videos with accurate GPS metadata.
• To display maps, addresses, weather, and location‑based gallery sorting.
• To improve app stability through diagnostics.
• To sync data to your chosen cloud provider (only if enabled).

4. Permissions We Request
• CAMERA — required to capture photos and videos.
• ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION — required for precise GPS tagging.
• ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION — for approximate location when high accuracy is unavailable.
• STORAGE (optional depending on Android version) — to save and access media.
• BACKGROUND_LOCATION (only if used) — to tag location when capturing through background services.

5. Data Storage & Retention
• Local data stays on your device unless you enable cloud sync.
• We retain analytics and crash reports for up to 6–12 months.
• You may delete photos/videos anytime.

6. Data Sharing
We do not sell personal data. We may share limited data with:
• Cloud storage providers — only if you enable sync.
• Analytics & crash-reporting services — for diagnostics.
All third parties are required to follow strict data protection standards.

7. Security
We use encryption in transit (HTTPS), secure storage practices, and strict access controls. No system is 100% secure, but we continuously work to protect your data.

8. Your Rights
• Access, modify, or delete data stored locally.
• Disable location or camera permissions anytime in system Settings.
• Request deletion of analytics data via email.

9. Children’s Privacy
The app is not designed for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

10. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

11. Contact Us
For questions or requests: support@yourdomain.com
